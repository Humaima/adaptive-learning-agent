Introduction to Cell Biology

Week 1: Cell Structure
Overview of the cell as the basic unit of life. Organelles (nucleus, mitochondria,
endoplasmic reticulum, Golgi apparatus), the cell membrane, and cytoplasm. The
difference between prokaryotic and eukaryotic cells.

Week 2: Cell Division
Mitosis and meiosis — how cells replicate and how genetic material is distributed
to daughter cells. This week assumes a basic understanding of DNA and genetics
from prior coursework.

Week 3: Cellular Respiration
How cells convert glucose and oxygen into usable energy (ATP) through glycolysis,
the citric acid cycle, and the electron transport chain. Builds directly on the
organelle structure covered in Week 1 (particularly mitochondria).

Week 4: Photosynthesis
How plant cells convert light energy into chemical energy. Draws a direct
comparison to cellular respiration from Week 3, since photosynthesis and
respiration are complementary processes.
